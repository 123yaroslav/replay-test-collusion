In the full instructed cartel (30 games x 30 hours), followers' logged asks depart from their channel-free replays almost only in the hours after the leader changes the floor (mean gap $0.42 against $0.05 in other hours; difference 95% CI +0.29 to +0.45): the replays keep the previous floor from memory, the logged asks follow the new message. The pre-registered joint bet loses evidence in exactly those hours (-0.035 bits per hour), the likelihood-ratio bet gains a little (+0.070, CI +0.007 to +0.144), and neither reaches an alarm; a co-departure statistic on the same exact null does (18 of 30 cartel games against 1 of 30 without chat; chosen after seeing the data).
